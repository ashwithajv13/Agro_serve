How the suitability score is calculated

Each crop gets up to 100 points: Soil 30 + Season 30 + Rainfall 20 + Water 20. Soil and season score full or zero. Rainfall loses 1 point for every 25 mm outside the crop's ideal range. Water scores 20 for an exact match, 10 if one level off and 0 if two levels off. 80+ Highly Suitable, 50-79 Suitable, below 50 Less Suitable.
